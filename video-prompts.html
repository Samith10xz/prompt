Video prompts that hold the shot.

UGC ads, Reels, product demos and explainers — with the hook in the first 2 seconds, the camera and light named, and every clip continuing seamlessly from the one before it.

Start with one sentence

What a good video prompt always names

  • Hook — problem first, bold claim, question, before / after, or story.
  • Camera — iPhone selfie, handheld, tripod, drone or close-up.
  • Lighting — natural imperfect light beats perfect studio light for UGC.
  • Duration — 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, and how the clips split.
  • Continuity — same person, same place, same look; no transitions, the next clip starts on the frame the last one ended on.
