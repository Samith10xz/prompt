(03) — Image Prompt Generator

Image prompts that look shot, not generated.

Product hero shots, social ads and lifestyle photos — with the background colour, the light, the format and the mood all named, so the result comes back clean and on-brand.

Start with one sentence

What a good image prompt always names

  • Style — photorealistic, cinematic, studio, editorial, minimal or vibrant.
  • Format — 1:1 square, 9:16 vertical, 16:9 landscape or 4:5 portrait.
  • Lighting — natural, studio softbox, golden hour or dramatic.
  • Mood — bold, warm, premium, playful, calm or luxury.
  • What to avoid — distorted hands, garbled text, watermarks, extra objects.
