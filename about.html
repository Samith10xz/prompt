(01) — About Us

Built by a studio that lives in prompts.

Graphix Prompt Studio is the in-house prompt tool of Graphix Design Agency, an independent design studio in Colombo, Sri Lanka — brand, motion, web and search, under one roof.

Why we built it

Every day we write prompts for clients: UGC videos, product shots, social posts, SEO briefs. The difference between a vague prompt and a detailed one is the difference between a usable result and another round of fixes. So we built the tool we wanted for ourselves — describe the job in one sentence, direct it with clicks, and get a full, detailed prompt built to spec.

How it works

  1. Pick the kind of work — image, video & UGC, social copy, SEO & content, brand & logo, or chat prompt.
  2. Write one sentence about what you need done, and who it is for.
  3. Click the direction chips — hook, camera, lighting, style, duration and more.
  4. Generate — you get a full detailed prompt, a negative prompt, three variations and tips. Copy it, save it, or check it as an image in the Member Area.
