How to write a UGC video prompt that actually looks like UGC

Name the camera (iPhone selfie), the light (natural, imperfect), the exact product words — and what not to show. The Maharaja video works because the brief says packet only, no cooking, no redesign. Vague prompts give vague videos; one named detail per line gives a usable one.

22 November 2026 · Prompt tips

Image prompts: name the background, the light and the format

A hero shot prompt that only says “premium product photo” will come back generic. Add the background colour, the light, the format (1:1, 9:16, 16:9) and the mood — and a short “avoid” list: no distorted hands, no garbled text, no watermark.
